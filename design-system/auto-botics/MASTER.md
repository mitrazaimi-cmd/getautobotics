Balance: White ~60% • Navy ~25% • Blue ~10% • Cyan ~5%.

**Contrast rules (mandatory):**
- Primary button label: **19px bold** white on `blue` → qualifies as WCAG large text (14pt bold), so 4.34:1 passes.
- Inline links in body copy: navy text + blue underline (never 16px blue text).
- Cyan is exposed only as `stroke-cyan` / `fill-cyan` / `bg-cyan` / `border-cyan` in practice; `text-cyan` only inside navy bands.

### Typography

- **Font:** Inter (via `next/font/google`, self-hosted, `display: swap`)
- **Headlines:** 700 / 600, tight tracking (-0.02em), line-height 1.1–1.2
- **Subheads:** 500
- **Body:** 400, 16–18px, line-height 1.6, max ~68ch
- **Scale:** 14 / 16 / 18 / 20 / 24 / 30 / 36 / 48 / 60

## Component Specs

### Buttons
- **Primary:** bg `blue`, white 19px bold label, padding 14px 24px, radius 8px, min-height 48px; hover `blue-hover`; 200ms color transition; no scale/translate.
- **Secondary:** transparent, navy 16px semibold text, 1.5px navy border; hover bg `surface`.
- **Text link CTA:** navy 16px semibold, blue 2px underline offset 4px, arrow icon that nudges 2px on hover.
- Focus: 3px `blue` outline, 2px offset — on every interactive element.

### Cards (brand frame C)
- White bg, radius **8px**, 1px `line` border, padding 24px (≥16px).
- Icon (Lucide 24px, stroke 1.75, navy) + label always visible.

### Callout
- `tint` background, **3px `blue` left bar**, radius 0 8px 8px 0, padding 16px 20px.

### Inputs
- Visible `<label>` above, 16px, min-height 48px, 1px `field` border, radius 8px.
- Focus: border `blue` + 3px `blue` outline offset 1px.
- Error: border `danger`, message below with icon + text, linked via `aria-describedby`.
- Required fields marked with "(required)" text; optional with "(optional)".

### Graphic elements (brand §09 continued)
- **Node Grid:** SVG dots + right-angled connectors, navy 10% on white or white 15% on navy, **one** cyan path. `aria-hidden`. Never behind body text.
- **Orbit Line:** 1.5px arc + end dot, cyan or blue. **Max one per layout** (used under the Method heading).

## Style Guidelines

**Style:** Accessible & Ethical + Minimalism & Swiss — clean, spacious, grid-based, high contrast, restrained effects.

### Page Pattern — Trust & Authority + Conversion (adapted)

- **Section order:** Hero (tagline + promise) → Problem/Outcome → Services → **Method** (replaces "Proof: logos/stats" — brand §12 forbids invented proof) → Portfolio (placeholders) → About → Book → Contact
- **CTA placement:** Primary `Book an automation consultation` in hero, after Services, after Method, About; `Book a consultation` persistent in header.
- **Conversion:** Low-friction form, clear next steps, transparency about process. No badges, logos, or stats until they are real.

---

## Motion

Standard tier, CSS only (no GSAP): reveal = opacity 0→1 + translateY 12px→0, 400ms `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)`, stagger 60ms, triggered once by IntersectionObserver. Hover transitions 150–200ms. **All disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`**, content rendered in final state (and visible without JS).

---

## Anti-Patterns (Do NOT Use)

- ❌ Humanoid robots, glowing brains, neon, sci-fi imagery (brand §10)
- ❌ AI purple/pink gradients
- ❌ Invented logos, testimonials, metrics, certifications (brand §12)
- ❌ Hype words: revolutionary, disruptive, game-changing, 10x, magic, cutting-edge, leverage, synergy
- ❌ Generic CTAs ("Click here", "Learn more")
- ❌ Cyan text on white; 16px Electric Blue text
- ❌ Emojis as icons; icon-only meaning without text labels
- ❌ Layout-shifting hovers; instant state changes; invisible focus
